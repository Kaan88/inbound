Inbound Download Manager Bridge

Hands browser downloads to Inbound Download Manager. The desktop app keeps the download list.

Integration

Automatic sending: checking…

When this is on, downloads that match your rules stop in your browser and start again in the desktop app, and they leave the browser's download list. For each one, the extension gives the app the file's address, the address of the page it came from, your browser's user agent, and your cookies for that site, so the app can download files that need your own login. The app keeps these on this computer with the download, the cookies encrypted, until you remove it, and sends the cookies only to that site. The address can also stay in the app's list of recent addresses. Nothing is sent to the developer. Privacy policy

"Ask each time" opens a small window for each caught download, starting at the app's default folder, with Choose… for another folder and Keep in browser to leave it to the browser. The folder you choose there is for that download only. The right-click menu always asks.

File and site rules

To keep a single download in your browser, hold Delete while you click its link. To keep them all for a while, or for buttons and links inside embedded frames where holding Delete does not work, use Pause catching in the toolbar popup. The extension's script on each page checks only whether Delete is held; it does not record what you type.

Access to sites

This browser has not given the extension access to the sites you download from, so it cannot read a site's cookies for the app or notice Delete held on a page. Downloads that need your login may fail in the app until you allow it.

Connection

This extension needs the Inbound Download Manager desktop app for Windows, and sends download details only to that app on this computer. Using the extension starts the app's background process if it is not running. If the app is not installed or cannot be reached, or it refuses a download, the download stays in your browser. Once the app has accepted one, the browser's copy is gone, so pause catching first for a file from a submitted form or a link that works only once.
