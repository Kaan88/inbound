What is sent

Sending a download gives the desktop app its address, the page it came from, your browser's user agent and your cookies for that site, so the app can download files that need your own login. The app keeps these on this computer with the download, the cookies encrypted, until you remove it, and sends the cookies only to that site. The address can also stay in the app's list of recent addresses. Sending a download to your server gives it the same details: the address, the page it came from, your browser's user agent and your cookies for that site. The server keeps them with the download, the cookies encrypted, until you remove it there, and sends the cookies only to that site.
